A building that never closes has no off-hours to recover the cost of power.

Horseshoe Indianapolis runs 8,760 hours a year, four times the hours of an office, so every overspent kilowatt is paid for around the clock. We cut that cost while the floor, the air, and the power stay exactly as they are.

The problem

Every overspent kilowatt bills you three times.

An office plant rests two thirds of the year. Yours does not rest at all.

1

The meter

Cooling plants, air handlers, and fans run every hour of the year. Seventeen years in, coils foul and controls drift, and there is no quiet night to claw it back.

2

The demand charge

One quarter hour sets the demand charge for the whole month. On a casino, the floor, the kitchens, and the cooling plant all peak together on a summer evening.

3

Equipment life

At 8,760 hours a year, a chiller reaches in about four years the run hours an office unit reaches in about seventeen. Replacement costs more with every cycle.

Energy distortion

Your floor sends distortion back into the building's power.

More than 1,500 slot machines, variable speed drives, LED drivers, UPS units, and other electronics push distortion back into the electrical system. It lands in three places.

1

The bill

Distortion is power you pay for that does no work. It becomes losses and heat in the wiring, transformers, and motors.

2

Maintenance

Nuisance trips, overheated panels and neutrals, failed boards, and extra service calls all trace back to it.

Up to 40%less maintenance

3

Equipment life

Transformers, motors, and electronics run hotter and wear out early.

5 to 15additional years of equipment life

The plan

Cool it. Clean it. Store it.

Cool

We restore and stage the chillers and air handlers, add variable speed drives to fans and pumps, and match the outside air a smoking floor requires to the crowd, without compromising air quality. The air stays as clean as you require. What changes is what it costs to make it so.

Clean

We correct distortion at the source, on the circuits that create it. Less power is lost as heat, and the panels, transformers, and motors downstream run cooler.

Store

Battery storage shaves the peaks that set the demand charge. It also stands behind the generators LionHeart already maintains, so the floor stays powered. The project is paid for from savings, and the federal storage tax credit and utility incentives are tailwinds where they apply.
